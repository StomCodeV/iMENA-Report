About the Platform

Designed for Student-Teachers

iMENA Report is an independent academic report preparation and formatting tool. It is not an official platform of MINEDUC, NESA, REB, TTC Muhanga or any host school. Students remain responsible for the accuracy and academic quality of their final report.

📝

Structured Workspace

Enter your own information, organise report sections in the correct order, and review your work before export.

📎

Supporting Documents

Upload your Schemes of Work, Sample Lesson Plan, and Teaching Timetable. These are preserved and included as appendices.

📄

Formatted PDF Export

Generate a clean, academic PDF ready for submission. No watermarks, no logos—just your hard work, formatted professionally.

🔄

Consistency Checks

The platform flags contradictory dates, subject/class mismatches, and missing sections—so you can correct them before submitting.

The Founder

Meet the Mind Behind iMENA Report

TUYISENGE Jean Chrisostom

Founder & Idea Generator — iMENA Hub

Jean Chrisostom is a final-year student teacher in Language Education at TTC Muhanga and a self-taught developer. He founded iMENA Hub to train student teachers across Rwanda in digital skills, believing every future teacher deserves digital confidence. iMENA Report is one of his many initiatives—a free tool built to make academic reporting professional, consistent, and stress-free for student-teachers.
